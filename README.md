# US Hiring Transparency Toolkit

A practical, privacy-conscious toolkit for U.S. job applicants requesting existing hiring-related personal information, asking about automated screening, and seeking voluntary rejection feedback.

Inspired by [girshovich/docs GDPR prompt](https://github.com/girshovich/docs/blob/main/GDPR.md). This is a U.S.-specific adaptation, **not** a claim that GDPR applies to every U.S. application.

## Quick start
1. Record the employer, position, date, application email, your residence, and where the employer operates.
2. Review [STATE_MATRIX.md](STATE_MATRIX.md) and [US_PRIVACY_RIGHTS.md](US_PRIVACY_RIGHTS.md) before asserting a legal right.
3. If an applicable access right is identified, customize [REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md](REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md). Otherwise use [REJECTION_FOLLOWUP.md](REJECTION_FOLLOWUP.md) as a voluntary request.
4. Ask separately about automated screening using [AI_SCREENING.md](AI_SCREENING.md).
5. Track outcomes in `TRACKER_TEMPLATE.csv`; do not commit filled-in records or personal data to a public repository.

## Limitations
U.S. privacy rights are state-specific and subject to eligibility, exclusions, exemptions, and changes. There is no universal federal applicant right to all recruiter notes, scores, or rejection explanations. Even an applicable access law may exclude confidential evaluations, trade secrets, other people's data, or certain employment records. Do not assume every automated hiring decision is unlawful or subject to a disclosure obligation.

This is educational information, not legal advice. Verify current official statutes and regulator guidance before sending a formal legal demand.
