Use only after confirming a specific applicable statute. Replace bracketed text; remove any request that exceeds the law's scope.

Subject: Personal information access request — [position] — [applicant name]

Hello Privacy Team,

I applied for [position] on [date] using [application email]. I am requesting access to personal information about me in connection with this application under [verified applicable law and section].

To the extent covered by that law, please provide existing applicant-related personal information, including application profile and status history; notes or assessments about me; applicant-specific ratings, scores, tags, or classifications; recorded decision or disposition codes; and information about the categories, sources, recipients, and retention of such information.

Please also tell me how to submit any identity verification needed to process this request securely. I understand applicable exemptions and third-party privacy protections may limit disclosure. If you cannot fulfill part of this request, please identify the relevant category and reason to the extent required by law.

Thank you,
[Name]
[Application email]

**Safety:** Do not send Social Security numbers, identity documents, or passwords in an initial email. Use an official secure verification process if required.
