# Questions about automated hiring systems

These are questions, **not assertions of a legal right** to source code or proprietary model logic.

- Was my resume parsed or screened using automated software?
- Were any applicant-specific scores, categories, or recommendations generated and retained?
- Was an automated threshold used to reject or advance my application?
- Did a person review my application before the rejection decision?
- Is there a published applicant notice describing the technology and any applicable audit or accommodation process?
- If relevant law applies, how may I exercise any available access, correction, appeal, or accommodation rights?

Some jurisdictions have specialized automated employment decision tool rules; assess their actual geographic and factual scope. NYC Local Law 144 is not a nationwide AI-screening disclosure law.

Avoid requesting trade secrets or claiming a universal right to algorithmic explanations.
