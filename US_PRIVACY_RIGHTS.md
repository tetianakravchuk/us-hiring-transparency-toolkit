# U.S. applicant privacy rights: decision tree

1. Identify your state of residence, the employer's relevant locations, and the specific processing at issue.
2. Determine whether the relevant state consumer privacy law covers the organization and the applicant's employment-context data. **Many state consumer privacy statutes exempt employment-context information.**
3. If covered, verify what personal data can be accessed, applicable exceptions, verification process, response timelines, and appeals.
4. If not covered, ask voluntarily for application data and feedback; do not claim a statutory entitlement.
5. Separately assess discrimination or background-check concerns. If an employer used a third-party consumer report, the Fair Credit Reporting Act may require particular notices and disclosures, depending on circumstances. It does not apply to every ATS score.
6. For suspected discrimination, consult the EEOC or the appropriate state agency promptly; charge-filing deadlines may apply. A privacy request does not automatically suspend those deadlines.
