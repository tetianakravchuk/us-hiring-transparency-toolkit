# Employer identification and jurisdiction mapping

Identify and independently verify:
1. Hiring entity's full legal name (not just brand).
2. Applicant residence and job location, including remote-state eligibility.
3. Headquarters and relevant offices (context only; not a legal eligibility shortcut).
4. Official careers posting and employer applicant privacy notice.
5. Official privacy contact or request portal; do not guess addresses.
6. Vendor involved in assessment or background reporting, if disclosed.
7. Evidence URLs, dates checked, conflicting claims, and unresolved facts.

Output jurisdiction candidates with confidence and reasons. Do not automatically use headquarters law, and do not claim that a company with offices in a state is necessarily subject to every provision of that state's law.
