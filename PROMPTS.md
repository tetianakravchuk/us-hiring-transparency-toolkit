# Reusable AI prompt

I applied to [company] for [role] on [date], from [state of residence], using [application email]. I received [rejection stage/date].

1. Find the employer's **official** privacy contact or applicant privacy portal, with source URLs; do not invent an address.
2. Research whether an actual legal right to applicant personal data applies, checking employment-context exclusions, employer thresholds, territorial scope, exemptions, and current regulator guidance. Cite official sources and label uncertainty.
3. If verified, draft a narrow access request under the correct statute. Otherwise draft a polite voluntary request without asserting a legal right.
4. Ask separately whether automated screening was used without assuming it was.
5. Provide recipient, subject, email body, and a short explanation of limits. Never invent rejection reasons or promise access to internal notes.
6. Do not suggest sending SSNs or identity documents by email.
