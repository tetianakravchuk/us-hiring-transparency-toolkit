# Example: Massachusetts resident applying to a U.S. remote job

**Scenario:** An external applicant living in Massachusetts is rejected for a remote AI evaluation role.

Do not assume:
- The California CCPA applies just because the employer has a California headquarters.
- Massachusetts employee personnel-record access applies to an unsuccessful external applicant.
- An ATS rejection automatically triggers an AI explanation right.

Recommended starting point: send a voluntary recruiter follow-up asking for feedback, recorded assessment results the employer is willing to share, and whether automated screening was used. If a covered third-party background report was involved, separately assess FCRA rights. Verify any other applicable jurisdiction-specific law before making a formal demand.

Keep applicant names, email addresses, and response documents out of public GitHub.
