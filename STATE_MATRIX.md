# State eligibility screening (not an exhaustive 50-state legal survey)

| Jurisdiction | Employment-applicant caution | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| California | California privacy law generally covers employee and applicant personal information, subject to exemptions and business coverage thresholds. | Check CPPA guidance and employer applicability before requesting access. |
| Massachusetts | No general GDPR-style right for every rejected applicant to obtain internal hiring evaluations. | Request feedback voluntarily; assess any specific applicable legal route. |
| Colorado | Colorado's consumer privacy law generally excludes individuals acting in an employment context. | Do not assume applicant-record access under the CPA. |
| Connecticut | Consumer privacy law generally excludes individuals acting in an employment context. | Do not assume applicant-record access under the CTDPA. |
| Other states | Coverage and employment exclusions vary; statutes change. | Verify the current official law, residency rules, covered-business thresholds, and exemptions. |

**Do not automatically apply the law of the employer's headquarters to an out-of-state applicant.**
